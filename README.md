# Vantage

Portfolio manager: one place to keep and look through your holdings, trades, events and news.

https://vantage.personal-553.workers.dev

## Features

- **Watchlist** — companies in your own groups, priced live. Charts from 1 day to 5 years as an overview grid or detailed cards, or a list of every horizon side by side. Prices in EUR, USD or each listing's own currency.
- **Portfolio** — holdings from your broker statement, priced live, by group, with cash and net value.
- **Trades** — every fill, grouped into round trips, with P&L by period, by name and per close.
- **Calendar** — earnings, dividends and other dated events by month, week or as a list.
- **News** — articles about the companies you follow, with your agent's read on each.
- **Tools** — margin bet sizing from your latest statement.
- **Agent API** — an agent can create your account, then read and write all of the above through the REST API, including your broker statements and fills. You can make and revoke agent keys.
